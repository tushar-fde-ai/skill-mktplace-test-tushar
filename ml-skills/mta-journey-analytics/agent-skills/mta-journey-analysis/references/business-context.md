## Business Model Overview

Treasure Bikes is a cycling and outdoor sports retailer selling **Bikes, Helmets, and Accessories** through three sales channels:

### Online (Website & Mobile App)
- **Website:** Primary digital storefront — highest traffic and order volume
- **Mobile App:** Growing channel for on-the-go purchases and loyalty engagement

### In-Store (Physical Retail)
- Brick-and-mortar locations for hands-on product experience, fitting, and service
- Sales rep interactions (email, call, in-person, survey) drive upsell and retention
